About

Who
I Am

I am a dual degree student at IIIT Hyderabad, pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science along with an MS in Computational Natural Sciences. I qualified for the Indian National Mathematical Olympiad in 2022 among the ~300 students selected nationally and attended the INMO Training Camp at ICTS-TIFR, Bengaluru.

ForgeLM: LLM Privacy Firewall
A local firewall that redacts personal data and secrets before text reaches any cloud LLM, using a transformer encoder that tags each token, served by a custom C++ inference engine with AVX2 kernels. 02
Distributed Systems · IICPC Finalist
AlphaExchange: Distributed Benchmarking Platform
A 6-service Go platform where users submit custom order-matching engines, each driven by 500 concurrent bots across 4 trader personas and ranked live on throughput, p99 latency, and correctness together instead of speed alone. Every fill is checked against an authoritative shadow order book that catches impossible executions a per-message check misses under reordering, so a fast but incorrect engine cannot outrank a correct one. Each untrusted submission runs in a locked-down container (seccomp, AppArmor, dropped capabilities, read-only non-root filesystem), so a hostile engine cannot reach the host or another contestant's run. Finalist, IICPC Summer Trading Hackathon 2026.

03
Research
Flash Crash Dynamics in Networked Oscillators
Models flash crashes as explosive synchronization of herding traders whose pull toward their neighbours grows with local consensus, so the market tips abruptly into a crash and stays locked there. Derives how the crash point shifts when each trader follows only k others, predicting a 1/k scaling and explaining why the standard mean-field estimate overshoots it on sparse networks. Large-scale simulations of 20,000-trader networks on cloud machines closely match the predicted shift and rule out the mean-field estimate.
